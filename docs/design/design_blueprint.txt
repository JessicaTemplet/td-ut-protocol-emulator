let p_fifo_no_deadlock ~clk ~rst ~full ~empty ~push ~pop =
  let open Hardcaml.Signal in
  (* Property 1: Full and Empty cannot be simultaneously asserted *)
  let p_valid_states = not_ (full &&& empty) in
  (* Property 2: If FIFO is full, Core 1 can always drain it within 4 cycles *)
  let p_liveness = always (implies full (eventually (~:full))) in
  p_valid_states &&& p_liveness

## As-Built Revision Notes (2026-09-30)

This blueprint is the original design. The design as built differs in the ways listed under "Deviations From Original
Design" in design_rationale.txt (deviations 1 to 7). In short:

- Target process is IHP CMOS5L (ihp-sg13cmos5l), not SG13G2. Hardcaml RTL is unaffected; physical design was redone.
- Instruction memory uses hard SRAM macros (two 512x8 per engine); the gate-count line item was replaced by measured areas.
- Instruction word: pin select is [13:11], a mode bit is [10]; payload counts prescaled ticks.
- The 8 shared pins are on the bidirectional uio bus with per-pin output enables (open-drain capable).
- Programs are loaded by a byte-serial loader (no address bus), not an SPI configuration interface.
- Only a pushing instruction stalls on a full FIFO.
- Autobaud edge capture exists as CAPTURE mode. Phase anchoring is a prescaled down-counter.
- Verification: directed simulation, bounded-exhaustive and SymbiYosys (unbounded) proofs of the FIFO, cocotb on the shipped
  Verilog and the hardened netlist. QuickCheck and Spike/QEMU co-simulation were not done.
- NOT yet built: hardware NRZI, bit-stuffing and CRC primitives; the glitch filter; a high-speed protocol demonstration.
  See ROADMAP.md.
